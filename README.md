# Awesome-Marine-Procurement

## Top Marine Procurement Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Maritime E-Procurement, Ship Chandlery Purchasing, Requisition-to-PO Workflows, Supplier Networks & Fleet Procurement*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Marine Procurement**. These systems enable ship operators, managers, and suppliers to digitize purchasing of spare parts, stores, provisions, and services—covering requisitions, RFQs, purchase orders, supplier networks, inventory integration, and compliance (including IHM where relevant).



**Examples** include ShipServ, Procureship, MESPAS Procurement, ShipNet Procurement, AMOS Procurement, DANAOS Procurement, Helm CONNECT Procurement, and related marine purchasing systems (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured marine e-procurement platforms with large supplier networks, EDI/trading connectivity, and fleet-scale workflows are almost exclusively commercial. Open-source activity is limited to broader ship-management systems that include procurement modules, chandler-oriented demos, and general open procurement/ERP tools. This section lists every relevant project and realistic building block found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[ShipServ](https://www.shipserv.com/)**  

  Leading maritime trading and e-procurement network connecting ship operators with suppliers through digital workflows, supporting high transaction volumes and supplier discovery.



- **[Procureship](https://www.procureship.com/)**  

  E-procurement platform optimized for the marine procurement cycle, with automation, supplier recommendation, integration to shipping systems, and marketplace connectivity.



- **[MESPAS Procurement](https://www.mespas.com/)**  

  Maritime software suite including procurement tightly linked to maintenance, inventory, and fleet operations, used by vessels and suppliers worldwide.



- **[AMOS Procurement, ShipNet Procurement, DANAOS Procurement, Helm CONNECT Procurement](https://www.specTec.com/)**  

  Procurement modules within established ship-management suites (SpecTec AMOS, ShipNet, DANAOS, Helm CONNECT) covering requisitions, POs, inventory, and shore–ship collaboration.



- **[Other marine purchasing & chandlery platforms](https://www.shipserv.com/)**  

  Additional solutions for marine stores, spare-parts sourcing, and digital purchasing workflows used by ship managers and operators.



- **[NauticExpo / related supplier directories](https://www.nauticexpo.com/)**  

  Supplier discovery and catalog platforms that complement formal procurement systems in the marine sector.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[SeaVesselManager](https://seavesselmanager.com/)**  

  Free open-source ship management software that includes a procurement module covering requisitions, purchase orders, approval chains, and supplier management, alongside maintenance, inventory, and certificates.



- **[ShipChandlerHub & similar chandler demos](https://github.com/ryantusi/ShipChandlerHub)**  

  Example web applications for ship chandlers featuring inventory (often IMPA-coded), order management, and basic vessel tracking—useful as reference implementations.



- **[Marine supply / chandlery marketplace experiments](https://github.com/search?q=ship+chandler+OR+marine+procurement+OR+yacht+parts)**  

  Prototype and hackathon projects for online marine supply ordering and parts marketplaces.



- **[General open-source procurement & ERP modules](https://github.com/search?q=procurement+OR+purchase+order+open+source+ERP)**  

  Open ERP and procurement systems (e.g., modules within Odoo, ERPNext, or similar) that can be adapted for vessel or fleet purchasing with custom maritime data models.



- **[Inventory & requisition open tools](https://github.com/search?q=inventory+requisition+OR+purchase+requisition)**  

  Libraries and simple applications for stock tracking, low-stock alerts, and basic purchase request workflows.



- **[IMPA / marine catalog helpers](https://github.com/search?q=IMPA+OR+marine+spare+parts+catalog)**  

  Community resources and data-handling scripts related to standard marine product coding and catalogs.



- **[Approval workflow engines](https://github.com/search?q=approval+workflow+open+source)**  

  Open workflow tools that can implement multi-level purchase approval chains typical in ship management.



- **[Integration & EDI-style connectors](https://github.com/search?q=EDI+OR+document+exchange+open+source)**  

  Open components for document exchange that may support custom buyer–supplier connectivity.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Ship-management suites with procurement**: SeaVesselManager as a starting point for smaller fleets seeking zero-license cost.

- **Chandler / supply demos**: Reference code for inventory and order handling around marine parts.

- **General ERP procurement**: Adapt open ERP purchase modules with vessel, voyage, and maritime supplier fields.

- **Custom stacks**: Inventory database + requisition forms + approval workflow + email/PO generation for basic digital purchasing.

- Fully featured marine e-procurement networks with thousands of suppliers and high transaction volume remain commercial.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

There is no mature, large-scale open-source equivalent to ShipServ, Procureship, or the procurement modules of MESPAS, AMOS, ShipNet, DANAOS, or Helm CONNECT.  

**SeaVesselManager** offers the most complete open ship-management foundation that includes procurement.  

General open ERP/procurement tools and small chandler-oriented projects provide additional building blocks.  

Commercial marine procurement platforms deliver supplier networks, standardized trading documents, IHM support, multi-vessel visibility, and integration with ship-management systems that most professional fleets require.  

Smaller operators or research projects may start with open ship-management or ERP tools; larger shipping companies typically adopt commercial e-procurement and integrated fleet systems.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Marine procurement involves safety-critical parts, regulatory requirements (including inventory of hazardous materials where applicable), and international supplier relationships. Incorrect purchasing or documentation can affect vessel compliance and operations.

- Open-source tools can support basic requisition and inventory workflows but generally lack the supplier network, trading standards, and operational maturity of commercial marine procurement platforms. Evaluate regulatory needs, integration requirements, and support carefully before production use on commercial vessels.
